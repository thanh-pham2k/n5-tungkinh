# Lesson 9 — Nghe trong ngữ cảnh và ôn tập

## Mục tiêu

Tom: It's my first day at the office. Is there anything I should know?

Anna: Yes. You must wear your ID card at work. You mustn't smoke inside the building.

Tom: Do I have to wear a suit?

Anna: No, you don't have to wear a suit. You can wear a shirt and trousers.

Tom: Great. I'm a little tired today.

Anna: You should get some coffee and take a short break.
