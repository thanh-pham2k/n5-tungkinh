# Lesson 10 — Phủ định với was / were

## Mục tiêu

Nắm vững và vận dụng **phủ định với was / were** trong câu tiếng Anh cơ bản.

---

## Phủ định với was / were

Cách tạo câu phủ định giống am / is / are: thêm not sau was / were.

> **Subject + was / were + not + ...**

Dạng rút gọn:

> was not → wasn't

> were not → weren't

### Ví dụ

I was tired.
→ Tôi đã mệt.

I wasn't tired.
→ Tôi đã không mệt.

He was at home.
→ Anh ấy đã ở nhà.

He wasn't at home.
→ Anh ấy đã không ở nhà.

They were busy.
→ Họ đã bận.

They weren't busy.
→ Họ đã không bận.

### So sánh

| Khẳng định | Phủ định |
| --- | --- |
| I was tired. | I wasn't tired. |
| She was at home. | She wasn't at home. |
| We were busy. | We weren't busy. |
| They were ready. | They weren't ready. |

Đây mới là phần **nhập môn**. Câu hỏi với was / were và Past Simple sẽ được học kỹ hơn ở bài sau.
