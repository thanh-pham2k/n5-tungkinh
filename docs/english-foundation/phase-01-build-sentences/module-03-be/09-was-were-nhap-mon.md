# Lesson 9 — Was / Were nhập môn

## Mục tiêu

Nắm vững và vận dụng **was / were nhập môn** trong câu tiếng Anh cơ bản.

---

## Was / Were nhập môn

was và were là dạng quá khứ của am / is / are.

So sánh:

| Hiện tại | Quá khứ |
| --- | --- |
| am | was |
| is | was |
| are | were |

### Cách chọn was / were

> **I / he / she / it → was**

> **you / we / they → were**

| Chủ ngữ | Quá khứ của be |
| --- | --- |
| I | was |
| he | was |
| she | was |
| it | was |
| you | were |
| we | were |
| they | were |

### So sánh hiện tại và quá khứ

**Hiện tại:**

I am tired today.
→ Hôm nay tôi mệt.

**Quá khứ:**

I was tired yesterday.
→ Hôm qua tôi mệt.

---

**Hiện tại:**

They are at home now.
→ Bây giờ họ ở nhà.

**Quá khứ:**

They were at home yesterday.
→ Hôm qua họ ở nhà.

### Dấu hiệu thời gian cơ bản

- now → bây giờ
- today → hôm nay
- yesterday → hôm qua
- last night → tối qua

Ví dụ:

She is busy today.
→ Hôm nay cô ấy bận.

She was busy yesterday.
→ Hôm qua cô ấy bận.

### Lỗi thường gặp

❌ I were tired yesterday.
✅ I was tired yesterday.

❌ She were at home.
✅ She was at home.

❌ They was busy.
✅ They were busy.
