# Lesson 4 — Đọc và tóm tắt bằng từ khóa và ôn tập

## Mục tiêu

Nắm vững và vận dụng **đọc và tóm tắt bằng từ khóa và ôn tập**.

---

## Đọc và tóm tắt bằng từ khóa

Bạn không cần chép lại đoạn văn.

Đọc:

> **Ryan wants to save money this year. He now makes coffee at home instead of buying it on the way to work. He also brings lunch from home four days a week. At weekends, he checks his shopping list carefully before going to the supermarket. These small changes help him spend less money each month.**

### Bước 1 — Ghi từ khóa

```text
Ryan
save money
coffee at home
bring lunch
shopping list
spend less
```

### Bước 2 — Tìm main idea

> **Ryan is changing some daily habits to save money.**

### Bước 3 — Tóm tắt

Một câu mẫu:

> **Ryan saves money by changing some of his daily habits.**

## Hoạt động viết ngắn

Đọc:

> **Sophie wants to be more active. She walks for thirty minutes after dinner four times a week. On Saturday mornings, she sometimes rides her bike in the park. She also takes the stairs instead of the elevator at work. These activities help her move more during the week.**

Không chép nguyên đoạn.

Hãy ghi:

```text
Main idea:
Detail 1:
Detail 2:
Detail 3:
```

### Gợi ý tự đối chiếu

<details>
<summary>Xem gợi ý</summary>

```text
Main idea: Sophie is trying to be more active.
Detail 1: She walks after dinner four times a week.
Detail 2: She sometimes rides her bike on Saturday.
Detail 3: She takes the stairs at work.
```

Một câu tóm tắt mẫu:

> Sophie uses simple daily activities to become more active.

</details>

Bạn làm tốt nếu:

- main idea bao quát cả đoạn;
- detail 1–3 đều có trong bài;
- không biến một chi tiết nhỏ thành main idea;
- không thêm thông tin không có trong đoạn.

## Ôn tập tổng hợp

### Đoạn 1

> **Daniel recently moved closer to his office. He used to spend almost an hour traveling to work, but now the trip takes only fifteen minutes. He usually rides his bike when the weather is good. When it rains, he takes the bus. Daniel likes his new apartment because his daily trip to work is much easier.**

### Đoạn 2

> **Emma is learning to cook more meals at home. On Sunday, she plans her meals for the week and buys the ingredients she needs. During the week, she usually cooks simple dishes after work.
