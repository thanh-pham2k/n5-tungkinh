## Paraphrase không cần giống từng từ

Trong bài đọc, câu hỏi thường không lặp nguyên văn câu trong đoạn.

### Đoạn văn

> **Sam usually walks to work because his office is only ten minutes from his apartment.**

Câu hỏi có thể hỏi:

> **Why doesn't Sam need to take a bus to work?**

Bạn không thấy nguyên câu trả lời trong bài.

Nhưng có thể suy ra:

```text
office
only 10 minutes from apartment
+
walks to work
↓
workplace is close to home
```

Đáp án có thể được diễn đạt:

> **Because he lives close to his workplace.**

Đó là paraphrase.

### Một ví dụ khác

Đoạn:

> **The store closes at nine every weekday.**

Câu hỏi:

> **When does the store stop serving customers from Monday to Friday?**

Ta cần nối:

```text
closes
≈
stops serving customers

weekday
≈
Monday to Friday
```

Đáp án:

> **At nine.**

### Lỗi thường gặp

Chỉ tìm đáp án có **đúng từ giống đoạn văn**.

Trong reading:

```text
same words
≠
always correct

same meaning
=
more important
```

> **Laura lives close to her office, so she usually walks to work. The trip takes about fifteen minutes. She enjoys walking because it gives her some exercise before she starts work.**

## Kết hợp từ tham chiếu và paraphrase

Khi đọc thực tế, nhiều kỹ năng xuất hiện cùng lúc.

### Đọc

> **Ben bought a bicycle last month because he wanted an easier way to travel around his neighborhood. He uses it to go to the supermarket and visit friends who live nearby. The bicycle was inexpensive, and Ben says it has already helped him use his car less often.**

### Bước 1 — Tìm tham chiếu

```text
He → Ben

it → the bicycle

him → Ben

his car → Ben's car
```

### Bước 2 — Nhận ra paraphrase

```text
nearby
≈
close to his home

inexpensive
≈
not expensive

use his car less often
≈
drive less
```

### Bước 3 — Tìm ý chính

> **Ben uses his new bicycle for short local trips.**

### Luyện tập

```quiz-deck

m21l2q29 | single_choice | It trong He uses it nói đến gì? | His car | His neighborhood | His bicycle | The supermarket | 3 | Ben sử dụng chiếc xe đạp để đi lại.
m21l2q30 | single_choice | Nearby gần nghĩa nhất với gì trong đoạn? | Far away | Close to where Ben lives | In another city | At Ben's workplace | 2 | Nearby chỉ những người bạn sống gần khu vực của Ben.
m21l2q31 | single_choice | Inexpensive có thể paraphrase thành gì? | Very popular | Not expensive | Very old | Easy to use | 2 | Inexpensive nghĩa là không đắt.
```

## Kết hợp context clues và paraphrase

### Đọc

> **Maya had a busy week at work, so she wanted a relaxing weekend. On Saturday morning, she stayed home and read a book. In the afternoon, she took a quiet walk in the park. On Sunday, she had lunch with her family and went to bed early. By Monday morning, she felt refreshed and ready to work again.**

Giả sử chưa biết `refreshed`.

Hãy dùng ngữ cảnh:

```text
busy week
↓
relaxing weekend
↓
rest + quiet activities + early sleep
↓
refreshed
↓
ready to work again
```

Có thể suy ra:

> `refreshed` ≈ rested and feeling better.

### Paraphrase

```text
wanted a relaxing weekend
≈
wanted to rest

felt refreshed
≈
felt rested and better
```
