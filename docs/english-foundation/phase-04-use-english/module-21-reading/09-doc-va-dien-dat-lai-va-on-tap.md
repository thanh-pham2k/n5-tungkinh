# Lesson 9 — Đọc và diễn đạt lại và ôn tập

## Mục tiêu

Nắm vững và vận dụng **đọc và diễn đạt lại và ôn tập**.

---

## Đọc và diễn đạt lại

Đọc:

> **Nina used to buy coffee on her way to work every morning. It cost about five dollars a day. She recently started making coffee at home instead. This small change saves her money and only takes a few minutes.**

### Bước 1 — Reference words

```text
It → buying coffee / the coffee
She → Nina
This small change → making coffee at home
her → Nina
```

### Bước 2 — Main idea

> **Nina now makes coffee at home to save money.**

### Bước 3 — Paraphrase

Original:

> **This small change saves her money.**

Paraphrase:

> **She spends less money because she makes coffee at home.**

## Hoạt động tự diễn đạt lại

Đọc:

> **Alex lives only a short walk from the supermarket. He usually goes there on Saturday morning and buys food for the week. The store is often busy at that time, so Alex tries to arrive early. This helps him finish his shopping more quickly.**

Hoàn thành:

```text
Main idea:
He → ?
there → ?
that time → ?
This → ?
busy ≈ ?
finish more quickly ≈ ?
```

### Tự đối chiếu

<details>
<summary>Xem gợi ý</summary>

```text
Main idea: Alex's Saturday shopping routine.

He → Alex

there → the supermarket

that time → Saturday morning

This → arriving early

busy ≈ crowded

finish more quickly ≈ need less time to finish
```

Một cách paraphrase:

> **Alex goes to the supermarket early on Saturday so he can complete his shopping faster.**

</details>

## Tóm tắt bằng từ của bạn

Khi tóm tắt, không cần thay mọi từ bằng synonym.

Mục tiêu là:

```text
giữ ý chính
+
bỏ chi tiết không cần thiết
+
dùng câu đơn giản
```

### Ví dụ

Original:

> **Emily works in an office about thirty minutes from her home. She used to drive every day, but parking was expensive. Now she takes the train. The station is close to both her home and office, and she spends less money on transportation.**

Không cần viết:

> Emily works in an office thirty minutes from home, used to drive, parking was expensive, takes the train, station is close...

Tóm tắt:

> **Emily now takes the train to work because it is more convenient and saves her money.**

### Nguyên tắc

```text
Passage
   ↓
Main idea
   ↓
Important details
   ↓
Remove minor details
   ↓
Simple summary
```
