# Lesson 9 — Bài nghe tổng hợp và ôn tập

## Mục tiêu

Nắm vững và vận dụng **bài nghe tổng hợp và ôn tập**.

---

## Bài nghe tổng hợp

### Trước khi nghe

Tìm:

```text
Where are they going?
When?
How?
What changes?
What is the final plan?
```

### Audio

`audio:en|A: What are you doing on Saturday? B: I'm visiting my parents. Do you want to come with me? A: Sure. Are you driving? B: I was going to drive, but my car isn't working. Let's take the train instead. A: Okay. What time does it leave? B: There's one at nine thirty. A: Great. Let's meet at the station at nine.`

`audio:en|A: I'd like a coffee, please. B: Small or large? A: Large, please.`

### Audio 2

`audio:en|A: Can we meet at ten? B: I have a class at ten. How about eleven thirty? A: That's fine.`
