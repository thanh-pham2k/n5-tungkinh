## Nghe câu trả lời ngắn

Nghe trước. Đừng mở transcript ngay.

`audio:en|I usually take the train to work because it is faster than the bus. My office is quite far from my home, so the train is more convenient for me.`

<details>
<summary>Xem transcript</summary>

I usually take the train to work because it is faster than the bus. My office is quite far from my home, so the train is more convenient for me.

</details>

### Điều cần nhận ra

Câu trả lời đi theo:

```text
ANSWER
I take the train.

↓

REASON
It is faster than the bus.

↓

DETAIL
My office is far from home.
```

## Tự nói theo khung

Chọn một câu hỏi:

### Daily life

> **What do you usually do after work?**

### Work

> **Do you like your job?**

### English

> **Why are you learning English?**

### Health

> **Do you exercise regularly?**

### Food

> **Do you usually cook at home?**

### Weekend

> **What do you like to do on weekends?**

Trả lời theo ba bước:

```text
1. ANSWER
↓
2. REASON
↓
3. DETAIL
```

### Ví dụ

Câu hỏi:

> **What do you like to do on weekends?**

Trả lời:

> **I like staying at home on weekends because it helps me relax. I usually cook, read, or watch a movie.**

### Checklist tự đối chiếu

```text
[ ] Tôi trả lời đúng câu hỏi trước.

[ ] Tôi có một reason rõ ràng.

[ ] Tôi dùng because đúng nếu cần.

[ ] Tôi có thể thêm một detail liên quan.

[ ] Tôi không chuyển sang chủ đề khác.

[ ] Tôi không cố nói quá dài.

[ ] Tôi có thể nói câu trả lời mà không đọc từng từ.
```

## Tự luyện nói với 5 câu hỏi

Trả lời mỗi câu bằng **2–3 câu tiếng Anh**.

### Question 1

> **What do you usually do in the evening?**

Gợi ý:

```text
Answer:
activity

Reason:
why?

Detail:
when / where / how long?
```

### Question 2

> **Why are you learning English?**

Gợi ý:

```text
Answer:
main reason

Detail:
work / travel / communication / study
```

### Question 3

> **Do you prefer cooking at home or eating out?**

Gợi ý:

```text
Answer:
choose one

Reason:
healthy / convenient / cheap / relaxing

Detail:
how often?
```

### Question 4

> **What do you like to do on weekends?**

Gợi ý:

```text
Answer:
activity

Reason:
why?

Example:
one typical weekend activity
```

### Question 5

> **How do you usually get to work or school?**

Gợi ý:

```text
Answer:
transport

Reason:
why?

Detail:
time / distance
```

### Tiêu chí

Không cần giống câu mẫu.

Mỗi câu trả lời đạt yêu cầu nếu có:

```text
clear answer
+
clear reason
+
optional useful detail
```
