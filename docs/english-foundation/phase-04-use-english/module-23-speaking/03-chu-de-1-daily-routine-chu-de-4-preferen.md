# Lesson 3 — Chủ đề 1 — Daily Routine — Chủ đề 4 — Preferences and Choices

## Mục tiêu

Nắm vững và vận dụng **chủ đề 1 — daily routine — chủ đề 4 — preferences and choices**.

---

## Chủ đề 1 — Daily Routine

Các câu hỏi thường gặp:

> **What time do you usually get up?**

> **What do you do in the morning?**

> **How do you get to work?**

> **What do you do after work?**

### Ví dụ

**Question**

> **What do you usually do in the morning?**

**Answer**

> **I usually have breakfast and drink coffee.**

**Reason**

> **I eat breakfast because I need energy for the morning.**

**Detail**

> **I usually eat at home before I go to work.**

Câu trả lời:

> **I usually have breakfast and drink coffee in the morning. I eat breakfast because I need energy. I usually eat at home before I go to work.**

## Chủ đề 2 — Work and Study

Câu hỏi:

> **Do you like your job?**

> **Where do you work?**

> **Why are you learning English?**

> **When do you usually study?**

### Ví dụ

> **Why are you learning English?**

> **I'm learning English because I want to communicate better at work. I usually study for thirty minutes after dinner.**

Hoặc:

> **Do you like your job?**

> **Yes, I do. I like my job because I can learn new things. My coworkers are also friendly.**

## Chủ đề 3 — Food and Health

Câu hỏi:

> **Do you cook at home?**

> **What food do you like?**

> **Do you exercise regularly?**

> **Why do you exercise?**

### Ví dụ

> **Do you cook at home?**

> **Yes, I do. I usually cook dinner because I want to eat healthier food. I also save money when I cook at home.**

> **Why do you exercise?**

> **I exercise because I want to stay healthy. I usually walk for thirty minutes after work.**

## Chủ đề 4 — Preferences and Choices

Khi được hỏi về lựa chọn, hãy:

```text
CHOOSE
↓
WHY?
↓
OPTIONAL DETAIL
```

### Ví dụ

> **Do you prefer coffee or tea?**

> **I prefer coffee because it helps me feel awake in the morning.**

> **Do you prefer working at home or at the office?**

> **I prefer working at home because it is quieter. I can focus better there.**

> **Would you rather take the bus or the train?**

> **I'd rather take the train because it is faster.**

### Một lỗi thường gặp

Không chỉ lặp lại lựa chọn:

> ❌ `I prefer coffee because I like coffee.`

Hãy đưa ra thông tin mới:

> ✅ `I prefer coffee because it helps me feel awake.`
