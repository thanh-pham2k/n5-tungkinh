# Lesson 5 — Nghe trong ngữ cảnh & Đọc và áp dụng

## Mục tiêu

Nắm vững và vận dụng **nghe trong ngữ cảnh & đọc và áp dụng**.

---

## Nghe trong ngữ cảnh

Nghe Anna nói về một ngày của cô ấy.

`audio:en|I usually get up at seven in the morning. I start work at nine. On Monday and Wednesday, I work at home. On Tuesday, I work at the office. My laptop is usually on my desk, and my phone is in my bag. I have lunch at noon. In the evening, I usually meet my friends at a small cafe.`

## Đọc và áp dụng

Đọc đoạn sau:

> Ben lives in a small apartment in the city. He gets up at 6:30 in the morning. On weekdays, he leaves home at 7:30. He usually arrives at work at 8:00. His office is on the third floor. His laptop is on his desk, and his lunch is in his bag. On Friday evening, Ben usually meets his friends at a restaurant.
