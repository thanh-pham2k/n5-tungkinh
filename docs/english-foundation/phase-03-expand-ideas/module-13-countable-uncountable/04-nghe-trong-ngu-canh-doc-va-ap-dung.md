## Nghe trong ngữ cảnh

## Đọc và áp dụng

Đọc đoạn sau:

> Tom wants to make breakfast for his family. There are four eggs in the fridge. There is some bread on the table, but there isn't any milk. Tom has some bananas and two apples. He wants to make coffee, but there isn't any coffee at home. Before breakfast, he goes to the supermarket to buy some milk and some coffee.
