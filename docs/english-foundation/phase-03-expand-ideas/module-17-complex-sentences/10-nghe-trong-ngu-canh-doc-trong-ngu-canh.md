## Nghe trong ngữ cảnh

Nghe Anna nói về một đồng nghiệp.

`audio:en|I work with a woman who is very good at her job. Her name is Lisa. She is the person who usually helps me when I have a difficult problem. I like working with her because she explains things clearly. For example, last week she helped me understand a new project. Lisa also uses a program that helps our team organize tasks. I think she is a great coworker.`

## Đọc trong ngữ cảnh

Đọc đoạn sau:

> David has a small café that is near my office. It is a place which is quiet and comfortable. I often go there because I can relax after work. For example, I sometimes read a book or study English there. David is the owner who usually works in the morning. He is friendly, and he remembers many of his customers. Although the café is small, it is one of my favorite places.
