## Vị trí mệnh đề và dấu câu

Cả bốn mệnh đề có thể xuất hiện trước mệnh đề chính.

```text
When I get home, I make dinner.

If I have time, I exercise.

Because I was tired, I went home.

Although it was raining, I went out.
```

Quy tắc cơ bản:

```text
Mệnh đề trạng ngữ đứng trước
             ↓
     dùng dấu phẩy
             ↓
Clause, main clause.
```

Khi mệnh đề trạng ngữ đứng sau:

```text
I make dinner when I get home.

I exercise if I have time.

I went home because I was tired.

I went out although it was raining.
```

Ở các mẫu cơ bản trên, thường không cần dấu phẩy.

### Hai cách viết

```text
Because I was tired, I went home.
                   ↓
                  comma


I went home because I was tired.
            ↓
       no comma needed
```

## Nghe trong ngữ cảnh

Nghe Mia nói về một ngày làm việc.

`audio:en|When I wake up, I usually check the weather. If it is sunny, I walk to work. If it rains, I take the bus. I usually bring lunch from home because food near my office is expensive. Although my job is sometimes busy, I enjoy it. When I finish work, I usually call my family or meet a friend.`
