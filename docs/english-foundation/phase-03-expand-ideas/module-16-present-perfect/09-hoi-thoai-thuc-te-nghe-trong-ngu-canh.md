# Lesson 9 — Hội thoại thực tế & Nghe trong ngữ cảnh

## Mục tiêu

Nắm vững và vận dụng **hội thoại thực tế & nghe trong ngữ cảnh**.

---

## Hội thoại thực tế

### Công việc

A: `How long have you worked here?`  
B: `I have worked here for three years.`  
A: `When did you start?`  
B: `I started in 2023.`

Chú ý:

```text
How long have you worked here?
→ khoảng thời gian kéo dài đến hiện tại
→ Present Perfect


When did you start?
→ thời điểm bắt đầu cụ thể
→ Past Simple
```

### Nơi ở

A: `How long have you lived in this apartment?`  
B: `I have lived here since January.`  
A: `Where did you live before?`  
B: `I lived with my parents.`

### Trải nghiệm

A: `Have you ever been to Singapore?`  
B: `Yes, I have.`  
A: `When did you go?`  
B: `I went there last year.`

Đây là pattern rất quan trọng:

```text
Have you ever...?
        ↓
hỏi trải nghiệm


When did you...?
        ↓
hỏi chi tiết quá khứ
```

## Nghe trong ngữ cảnh

Nghe Emma nói về công việc và nơi ở.

`audio:en|I have lived in this city for five years. I moved here in 2021 because I found a new job. I have worked at the same company since then. I started as an assistant, but I became a team leader two years ago. I have known some of my coworkers for five years. Last year, I moved to a new apartment near my office. I have lived there since last September, and I really like the area.`
