## Nghe trong ngữ cảnh

Nghe Mai nói về một ngày bình thường của cô ấy.

`audio:en|I usually get up at six thirty. I eat breakfast quickly and walk to work. I am usually early. At work, I speak clearly during meetings and check my work carefully. I sometimes eat lunch with my coworkers. In the evening, I usually read quietly at home.`

## Đọc và áp dụng

Đọc đoạn sau:

> David works in a small office. He is usually busy in the morning. He always checks his email before work. During meetings, he listens carefully and speaks clearly. He sometimes has lunch with his team. After work, he drives home slowly because the streets are often busy.
