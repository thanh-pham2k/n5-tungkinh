# Lesson 9 — Phát triển một đoạn văn ngắn & Nghe trong ngữ cảnh

## Mục tiêu

Nắm vững và vận dụng **phát triển một đoạn văn ngắn & nghe trong ngữ cảnh**.

---

## Phát triển một đoạn văn ngắn

Các từ nối giúp câu không còn đứng riêng lẻ.

Thay vì:

> My apartment is small. It is comfortable. It is near my office. I walk to work. There are useful places nearby.

Có thể phát triển:

> `Although my apartment is small, it is comfortable. It is also near my office. Therefore, I usually walk to work. There are many useful places nearby. For example, there is a supermarket across the street. The area is busy. However, I like living there.`

Các từ nối tạo quan hệ:

```text
small
  │
  │ ALTHOUGH — tương phản
  ↓
comfortable

near office
  │
  │ THEREFORE — kết quả
  ↓
walk to work

useful places
  │
  │ FOR EXAMPLE — ví dụ
  ↓
supermarket

busy
  │
  │ HOWEVER — tương phản
  ↓
like living there
```

## Nghe trong ngữ cảnh

Nghe Maya nói về công việc mới.

`audio:en|I started a new job last month. Although the job is sometimes difficult, I enjoy it. My office is far from my home. Therefore, I take the train every morning. The train is usually fast. However, it can be crowded. I use English at work every day. For example, I read emails and talk to international customers. I am often tired after work, but I am happy with my new job.`
