## Nghe trong ngữ cảnh

Nghe Emma nói về một ngày làm việc của mình.

`audio:en|I usually get up at seven and have breakfast at home. My office is far from my house, so I take the bus to work. The bus is cheap, but it is sometimes slow. I usually bring lunch because food near my office is expensive. I finish work at five, and I go home. I am often tired, so I relax for a while before dinner.`

## Đọc trong ngữ cảnh

Đọc đoạn sau:

> Ben lives in a small apartment near the city center. The apartment is small, but it is clean and comfortable. Ben likes living there because he can walk to work. He doesn't have a car, so he usually walks or takes the bus. There are many restaurants near his apartment, but he often cooks at home because eating out is expensive.
