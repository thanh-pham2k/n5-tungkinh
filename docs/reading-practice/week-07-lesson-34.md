# Lesson 34 — Ocean Exploration

Focus: Matching Features · Cause and Effect · Scientific Inference

### Reading Passage

Exploring the Ocean's Hidden Regions

A. Much of the ocean remains difficult to study directly because deep environments are dark, cold, and under enormous pressure. Scientists are interested in these regions because they contain unfamiliar habitats and may reveal how marine ecosystems function. However, gathering reliable information requires equipment designed for conditions that humans cannot easily tolerate.

B. Researchers use several methods to explore the ocean. Sonar systems send sound signals through water and measure returning echoes to estimate the shape of the seafloor. Remotely operated vehicles, or ROVs, are controlled from ships and can carry cameras and instruments into deep environments. Autonomous underwater vehicles, or AUVs, follow programmed routes and collect measurements without continuous direct control.

C. Each method provides different evidence. Sonar can produce maps of large underwater areas, but it does not necessarily reveal every small organism living there. An ROV can record detailed images of a particular location, although its observations may cover only a limited area. AUVs can survey selected routes efficiently, but scientists still need to interpret the measurements they return.

D. Discovering unfamiliar organisms may lead researchers to ask how those species survive. For example, an animal found in an extremely dark environment might have adaptations that help it obtain food without sunlight. Yet observing the animal does not immediately establish how every part of its biology works. Scientists may need repeated observations and comparisons with other species.

E. Ocean exploration is valuable because different tools reveal different parts of an environment. No single instrument provides a complete picture. Combining broad mapping with detailed observation helps scientists build explanations while recognizing what their evidence cannot yet show.

### Paraphrasing

- estimate the shape of the seafloor → produce an approximate map of the ocean bottom
- continuous direct control → constant human operation
- detailed images → close visual observations
- limited area → restricted geographical coverage
- complete picture → comprehensive understanding

### Common Mistakes

- Matching Features: Sonar tạo bản đồ, ROV quan sát chi tiết, AUV khảo sát theo tuyến tự động.
- Inference: Quan sát một sinh vật không đủ giải thích toàn bộ cơ chế sinh tồn.
- Cause and Effect: Hạn chế vùng quan sát của ROV là lý do cần kết hợp nhiều phương pháp.
