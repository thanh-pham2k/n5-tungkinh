# Lesson 07 — Social Media and Teenagers

Focus: Paraphrasing · Cause and Effect · True/False/Not Given

### Reading Passage

Connected but Not Always Close

A. Social media has become a common part of teenage life. Young people use digital platforms to exchange messages, share experiences, and follow topics that interest them. These services can strengthen friendships, particularly when people live far apart. However, the relationship between social media and teenagers' well-being is complex. The effects depend not only on the time spent online but also on the activities users choose.

B. Social platforms can provide opportunities for learning and creative expression. Teenagers may join communities focused on photography, languages, or science. Receiving feedback from others can encourage them to develop new skills. Yet the quality of online information varies, and popularity does not necessarily indicate that advice is accurate or useful.

C. One concern involves social comparison. Users often publish carefully selected moments rather than ordinary daily experiences. Teenagers who repeatedly compare their lives with these posts may develop unrealistic expectations. This can contribute to dissatisfaction, especially when they interpret other people's online images as complete representations of reality.

D. Another issue is the effect of notifications. Frequent alerts may interrupt homework or conversations, making sustained attention more difficult. Some teenagers respond by turning off unnecessary notifications or setting specific times to check applications. These strategies aim to reduce interruptions without requiring users to abandon social media completely.

E. Researchers and educators increasingly emphasize the importance of how teenagers use these platforms. Actively communicating with supportive friends may produce different experiences from endlessly viewing content without interaction. Instead of treating every hour online as equally harmful or beneficial, families can discuss online habits, privacy, and emotional responses. A balanced approach focuses on purposeful use rather than simple restrictions.

### Paraphrasing

- strengthen friendships → reinforce personal relationships
- carefully selected moments → selectively presented experiences
- unrealistic expectations → beliefs that do not reflect reality
- reduce interruptions → limit disruptions
- purposeful use → intentional engagement

### Common Mistakes

- Nhầm cause-effect: So sánh với hình ảnh lý tưởng có thể gây dissatisfaction, nhưng bài không khẳng định tất cả teenagers đều bị ảnh hưởng.
- Nhầm False và Not Given: Không có ngưỡng hai giờ được nghiên cứu trong passage.
- Chọn ý quá rộng: Thời lượng online là một yếu tố, không phải yếu tố duy nhất.
