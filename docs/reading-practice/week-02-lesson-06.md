# Lesson 06 — Artificial Intelligence in Daily Life

Focus: Paraphrasing · Cause and Effect · Identifying Main Ideas

### Reading Passage

The Invisible Assistant in Everyday Life

A. Artificial intelligence, commonly known as AI, is becoming part of everyday activities, often without users noticing it. When a music application suggests a song or a navigation system recommends a faster route, AI may be analysing information to make predictions. Unlike traditional programs that follow only fixed instructions, many AI systems identify patterns in data. However, their growing presence raises questions about how much people should depend on automated decisions.

B. One advantage of AI is its ability to process large amounts of information quickly. Online shops use recommendation systems to suggest products based on previous purchases or browsing behaviour. This can save customers time by reducing the number of options they need to examine. Nevertheless, recommendations may also limit discovery if users repeatedly see products similar to those they already prefer.

C. AI is also used in healthcare administration. Some hospitals employ automated systems to organize appointments and identify scheduling problems. These tools can reduce routine administrative work, allowing staff to spend more time assisting patients. Yet an automated system may make unsuitable suggestions when the information it receives is incomplete or inaccurate.

D. Privacy is another concern. Many AI services depend on personal information, including search histories and location data. Such information can improve predictions, but collecting it creates risks if companies fail to protect it properly. Users may not always understand what information is collected or how long it is stored.

E. AI therefore offers convenience without guaranteeing better decisions in every situation. Its value depends on data quality, responsible design, and human judgment. Rather than accepting every automated suggestion, people need to understand when technology can help and when additional checking is necessary.

### Paraphrasing

- process large amounts of information → analyse extensive data
- reducing the number of options → narrowing the available choices
- incomplete or inaccurate → missing or incorrect
- limit discovery → reduce exposure to unfamiliar options
- additional checking is necessary → further verification is required

### Common Mistakes

- Nhầm nguyên nhân và kết quả: Dữ liệu sai dẫn đến gợi ý không phù hợp, không phải ngược lại.
- Chọn theo từ khóa: AI xử lý nhanh không đồng nghĩa AI luôn chính xác.
- Suy luận quá mức: Bài nói có rủi ro privacy, không nói mọi công ty đều sử dụng dữ liệu sai mục đích.
