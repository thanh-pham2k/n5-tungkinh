# Lesson 05 — The Future of Schools

Focus: Main Idea · Matching Headings · Writer's View · Inference

### Reading Passage

What Might Tomorrow's Schools Look Like?

A. Schools have changed considerably over time, but classrooms still share many familiar features. Students gather with teachers, follow schedules, and complete assessments. As technology develops and employment patterns change, educators are reconsidering how schools should prepare young people for adulthood. Some predict major transformations, while others argue that essential features of education will remain.

B. One possible development is more personalized learning. Digital systems can offer exercises based on students' previous answers, allowing individuals to practise different skills at different speeds. This could help teachers recognize learning difficulties earlier. However, personalized software cannot fully understand a student's emotional needs or family circumstances. Teachers therefore remain important in interpreting information and providing support.

C. Future schools may also place greater emphasis on collaboration and problem-solving. Students could work together on projects involving environmental issues, local communities, or practical design challenges. Such activities encourage learners to apply knowledge from several subjects. Nevertheless, group projects need clear responsibilities and careful assessment; otherwise, some students may contribute much less than others.

D. School buildings themselves might become more flexible. Instead of using every room for traditional lectures, schools could create spaces for teamwork, independent study, and practical activities. Yet these changes require investment, and not every community has the same financial resources. Introducing expensive equipment without improving teaching practices may produce disappointing results.

E. Despite uncertainty about future technologies, many educators agree that schools will continue to serve a social purpose. They provide opportunities to build friendships, resolve disagreements, and participate in shared activities. The challenge is to adapt educational methods without losing these functions. The most successful schools may not be those with the newest devices, but those that use available resources to meet students' academic and social needs.

### Paraphrasing

- reconsidering how schools should prepare young people → reviewing educational approaches for future needs
- based on students' previous answers → adjusted according to earlier performance
- clear responsibilities → well-defined individual duties
- require investment → need financial support
- serve a social purpose → fulfil a role in developing relationships

### Common Mistakes

- Nhầm False với Not Given: Bài không nói thay toàn bộ kỳ thi trước 2030. Đây là thiếu thông tin, không phải thông tin bị phủ định.
- Nhầm ý chính: Công nghệ là một phần của tương lai trường học, không phải giải pháp duy nhất.
- Bỏ qua thái độ tác giả: Tác giả ủng hộ đổi mới có điều kiện, không hoàn toàn ủng hộ hoặc phản đối.
