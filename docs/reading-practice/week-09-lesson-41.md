# Lesson 41 — Online Shopping

Focus: Data and Details · Table Completion · Consumer Behaviour

### Reading Passage

How Online Shopping Changes Buying Habits

A. Online shopping has changed how consumers compare products and make purchasing decisions. Customers can examine prices, read reviews, and place orders without visiting physical shops. However, convenience does not always lead to better decisions. Some shoppers buy unnecessary products because discounts and recommendations make offers appear more attractive than they really are.

B. A fictional survey of 500 shoppers examined their main reasons for buying online. In 2022, 42% selected convenience, 30% selected lower prices, 18% selected product variety, and 10% selected other reasons. In 2025, convenience increased to 48%, while lower prices fell to 25%. Product variety rose slightly to 20%, and other reasons accounted for 7%.

C. Delivery expectations also influence behaviour. In the survey, 60% of respondents in 2025 said they would consider abandoning an order if delivery costs were unexpectedly high. This figure was 45% in 2022. The increase suggests that shoppers may pay more attention to the total cost of an order, not simply the advertised product price.

D. Reviews provide another source of information, but their influence is complicated. Positive comments may increase confidence, while repeated complaints about product quality can discourage purchases. Nevertheless, customers should consider whether reviews describe relevant experiences rather than relying only on average ratings.

E. Online retailers can attract customers through competitive prices and convenient services, but long-term trust requires accurate information and transparent charges. The survey suggests that consumer priorities can shift over time. Businesses that focus only on discounts may overlook concerns about delivery, product quality, and reliability.

### Paraphrasing

- main reasons for buying online → primary motivations for online purchases
- unexpectedly high delivery costs → unanticipated shipping charges
- abandoning an order → leaving without completing a purchase
- long-term trust → confidence maintained over time
- consumer priorities shift → customer preferences change

### Common Mistakes

- Percentage points: 42% lên 48% là tăng 6 điểm phần trăm, không phải tăng 6%.
- Nhầm năm: Lower prices năm 2022 là 30%, năm 2025 là 25%.
- Inference: Tỷ lệ chọn giá thấp giảm không có nghĩa giá cả hết quan trọng.
