# Lesson 15 — Sustainable Cities

Focus: Problem and Solution · Matching Information · Inference

### Reading Passage

Designing Cities for People and the Planet

A. As urban populations grow, cities face increasing pressure on housing, transport, water, and public spaces. Sustainable urban planning aims to meet residents' needs while limiting environmental damage. However, a city cannot become sustainable simply by installing modern technology or constructing attractive buildings. Planners must consider how different systems interact and who benefits from improvements.

B. Transportation is one important challenge. Heavy dependence on private cars contributes to traffic congestion and air pollution. Improving buses, trains, and safe cycling routes can provide alternatives. Yet these services must be reliable and affordable. A new railway line may have limited value to residents if reaching the nearest station is difficult or expensive.

C. Green spaces can help cities manage heat and rainfall. Trees provide shade, while parks and planted areas can absorb some rainwater that would otherwise flow rapidly into drainage systems. These benefits depend on maintenance and suitable design. A poorly maintained park may offer fewer environmental and social advantages than planners originally expected.

D. Waste and water management also require attention. Growing populations can increase demand for clean water and produce larger quantities of rubbish. Cities may respond through improved collection systems, water-saving equipment, and programmes that encourage residents to reduce waste. However, infrastructure improvements are less effective when people lack information or cannot access the services.

E. Sustainable development also involves fairness. Environmental improvements can make neighbourhoods more attractive, sometimes increasing housing costs. If lower-income residents can no longer afford to remain, the benefits may be distributed unevenly. Successful urban planning therefore requires long-term thinking, community participation, and policies that protect access to essential services.

### Paraphrasing

- heavy dependence on private cars → strong reliance on personal vehicles
- reliable and affordable → dependable and reasonably priced
- absorb some rainwater → retain part of the rainfall
- distributed unevenly → shared unequally
- protect access to essential services → ensure important services remain available

### Common Mistakes

- Nhầm giải pháp với điều kiện: Xây tuyến tàu mới không đủ nếu người dân khó tiếp cận nhà ga.
- Nhầm environmental sustainability với social sustainability: Thành phố xanh hơn vẫn có thể khiến một số cư dân chịu áp lực chi phí.
- Suy luận quá mức: Green spaces giảm một phần runoff, không bảo đảm hết ngập lụt.
