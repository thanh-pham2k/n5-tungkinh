# Lesson 45 — The Future of Money

Focus: Data and Details · Comparing Trends · Consumer Trust

### Reading Passage

How Payment Habits Are Changing

A. Payment methods have changed as consumers gain access to cards, mobile applications, and other digital services. Digital payments can make transactions faster and reduce the need to carry physical cash. Nevertheless, the future of money is unlikely to depend only on technology. Trust, accessibility, and personal preferences also influence how people choose to pay.

B. A fictional survey of 600 adults examined their most frequently used payment method. In 2022, 45% selected cash, 35% selected bank cards, and 20% selected mobile payments. In 2025, cash declined to 30%, bank cards remained at 35%, and mobile payments increased to 35%. Thus, mobile payments reached the same share as cards, while cash remained important for a substantial group.

C. Respondents gave different reasons for their choices. Many mobile-payment users valued speed and convenience. Some card users preferred a familiar method that worked across many shops. Cash users often mentioned simplicity or concern about relying on a phone and internet connection. These preferences show why one payment method may not suit every situation.

D. Security and access also affect adoption. Digital services can provide transaction records, but users may worry about fraud or technical failures. People without suitable devices or reliable connectivity may find cash more practical. Businesses that remove cash payments entirely could therefore make purchases more difficult for certain customers.

E. Payment systems are likely to continue evolving, but the survey does not establish that cash will disappear. A useful payment environment offers reliable choices and helps consumers understand costs and risks. The important question is not which method appears most modern, but whether people can pay safely and conveniently.

### Paraphrasing

- most frequently used → used more often than other methods
- declined to 30% → fell until it represented 30%
- remained unchanged → stayed at the same level
- reliable connectivity → dependable internet access
- maintaining payment choices → preserving alternative ways to pay

### Common Mistakes

- Equal share ≠ Same growth: Cards và mobile cùng 35% năm 2025, nhưng cards không tăng.
- False vs Not Given: Bài trực tiếp bác bỏ việc khảo sát chứng minh cash sẽ biến mất.
- Inference: Digital payments phát triển không có nghĩa mọi người đều sử dụng được.
