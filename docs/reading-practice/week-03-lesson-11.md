# Lesson 11 — Plastic Pollution

Focus: Matching Information · TFNG · Environmental Impact

### Reading Passage

The Long Journey of Plastic Waste

A. Plastic has become an essential material in modern life because it is lightweight, inexpensive, and durable. It is used in food packaging, medical equipment, and household products. However, the same durability that makes plastic useful also creates environmental problems. Many plastic items are discarded after only a few minutes of use, yet they may remain in the environment for decades or longer. This difference between a product's useful life and its persistence is a central challenge.

B. Plastic waste can enter rivers and oceans through several routes. Rubbish left on streets may be carried into drains during heavy rain, while poorly managed landfills can release waste into surrounding areas. Once plastic reaches waterways, currents may transport it far from its original source. Consequently, pollution discovered on a remote beach does not necessarily come from nearby communities.

C. Marine animals face several risks from plastic pollution. Some mistake floating fragments for food, while others become trapped in discarded fishing equipment. Over time, larger plastic objects can break into smaller pieces known as microplastics. Scientists have detected these particles in various environments, although their long-term effects on every species are not yet fully understood.

D. Recycling can reduce the demand for new materials, but it is not a complete solution. Different plastics require different processing methods, and contaminated packaging may be difficult to recycle. In some places, collection systems are also insufficient. Therefore, increasing recycling rates requires improvements in product design, waste separation, and local infrastructure.

E. Many environmental groups recommend reducing unnecessary plastic consumption before focusing on disposal. Reusable containers, refill systems, and alternative packaging can prevent some waste from being created. Nevertheless, alternatives must also be evaluated for their environmental costs. Effective action involves reducing avoidable waste while improving the management of materials that remain necessary.

### Paraphrasing

- remain in the environment → persist in natural surroundings
- carried into drains → washed into drainage systems
- original source → place where waste first entered the environment
- insufficient collection systems → inadequate waste-gathering infrastructure
- prevent waste from being created → reduce waste at its source

### Common Mistakes

- False vs Not Given: Bài nói chưa hiểu đầy đủ tác động của microplastics, nên câu khẳng định đã biết chính xác là False.
- Matching Information: Chọn đoạn theo ý chính, không chỉ vì có từ plastic hoặc environment.
- Suy luận quá mức: Giải pháp thay thế nhựa không mặc nhiên tốt hơn về mọi mặt môi trường.
