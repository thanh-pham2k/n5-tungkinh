## Passage A — The Problem with Sitting All Day

A. Many modern jobs and educational activities involve long periods of sitting. Office employees may spend hours at computers, while students attend lessons and complete assignments at desks. Sitting itself is a normal part of daily life, but remaining in one position for extended periods can reduce opportunities for movement. Some people also notice stiffness or discomfort after spending much of the day without changing position.

B. One practical response is to introduce short movement breaks. Employees might stand during certain conversations or walk briefly between tasks. Schools may encourage students to move during breaks rather than remaining seated throughout the day. These activities do not replace every benefit of regular exercise, but they can reduce uninterrupted periods of inactivity.

C. Workplace and school environments can influence whether people adopt these habits. A person may intend to move more often but forget when concentrating on demanding work. Reminders, suitable spaces, and supportive schedules can make movement easier. The broader challenge is to design routines that encourage activity without unnecessarily interrupting important tasks.

## Passage B — The Value of Cooking at Home

A. Preparing meals at home can give people greater control over ingredients, portion sizes, and cooking methods. It may also encourage families to spend time together. However, home cooking does not automatically produce a balanced diet. The nutritional quality of a meal still depends on the ingredients selected and how they are prepared.

B. Time is a common obstacle. People with long working hours may find daily cooking difficult, particularly when shopping and cleaning are included. Some households respond by preparing ingredients in advance or cooking several portions at once. These approaches can reduce the effort required on busy days, although they still demand some initial planning.

C. Cooking skills and confidence also matter. Beginners may avoid preparing unfamiliar dishes because they worry about making mistakes or wasting ingredients. Starting with simple recipes can help develop confidence gradually. Rather than expecting every meal to be elaborate, people may benefit from learning a small number of practical dishes that fit their schedules and dietary needs.

## Passage C — Finding Time for Rest

A. Rest is sometimes confused with doing nothing, but recovery can involve different activities. Some people relax by reading quietly, while others prefer walking, listening to music, or spending time with friends. The most suitable activity depends partly on what caused the tiredness. After a mentally demanding day, a change of activity may feel refreshing, whereas physical exhaustion may create a stronger need for quiet rest.

B. Digital entertainment is often used for relaxation because it is easy to access. However, watching videos or checking messages for longer than intended can reduce the time available for sleep or other activities. This does not mean digital entertainment is always unhelpful. The important issue is whether it provides the intended break without creating additional problems.

C. Establishing boundaries can support more effective rest. People might decide when to stop working, set aside periods without notifications, or plan enjoyable activities. Nevertheless, rest routines must remain realistic. A complicated schedule that creates extra pressure may defeat its original purpose. Effective recovery involves understanding personal needs and choosing manageable habits.

### Kỹ năng cần đạt

- Identifying Details: Xác định chính xác thông tin và đối tượng được nhắc tới, kể cả khi câu hỏi paraphrase.
- Summary Completion: Chọn cụm từ phù hợp cả ngữ pháp lẫn nội dung, không chỉ vì cùng chủ đề.
- Cause and Effect: Phân biệt nguyên nhân, kết quả và các yếu tố chỉ có thể ảnh hưởng.
- Inference: Suy luận từ passage mà không sử dụng kiến thức y khoa bên ngoài.
- True/False/Not Given: Phân biệt thông tin bị bác bỏ với thông tin không được cung cấp.

Mục tiêu luyện tập: Đạt 39–44/55 câu (70–80%). Khi kiểm tra bài, hãy xác định evidence cụ thể và giải thích tại sao từng distractor không phù hợp, đặc biệt ở các câu Summary Completion và Cause and Effect.
