# Lesson 27 — The Four-Day Workweek

Focus: Comparing Viewpoints · Writer's Attitude · Yes/No/Not Given

### Reading Passage

Can Fewer Working Days Produce Better Results?

A. The four-day workweek has attracted attention as organizations reconsider traditional schedules. However, the term does not always describe the same arrangement. Some employers reduce working hours without lowering salaries, while others expect employees to complete the same weekly hours across four longer days. These differences matter when evaluating the benefits of the policy.

B. Supporters of shorter working weeks argue that employees may return to work more rested and focused. They suggest that reducing unnecessary meetings and improving task organization could allow some teams to maintain their output in fewer hours. Employees might also gain time for family responsibilities or personal interests. Yet these arguments depend on whether workplaces can actually redesign their processes.

C. Critics point to industries where services must remain available throughout the week. Hospitals, transport providers, and customer-support teams may need additional staff or complicated schedules to maintain coverage. Some employers also fear that workers could experience greater pressure if expectations remain unchanged while available working time decreases.

D. Evidence from individual workplace trials can provide useful insights, but results require careful interpretation. A successful trial in a software company does not automatically establish that the same model will work in a small restaurant. Outcomes may depend on staffing levels, customer demand, and whether the trial measures productivity, employee satisfaction, or both.

E. The four-day workweek deserves serious consideration, but enthusiasm should not replace evaluation. Organizations need to define the proposed schedule clearly and examine its effects on employees, customers, and costs. The central question is not simply whether four days are better than five, but under which conditions a shorter working week can be effective and fair.

### Paraphrasing

- reconsider traditional schedules → review established working arrangements
- maintain their output → preserve productivity levels
- remain available throughout the week → provide continuous service
- careful interpretation → thoughtful analysis of findings
- under which conditions → in what circumstances

### Common Mistakes

- Nhầm hai mô hình: Bốn ngày làm việc không nhất thiết đồng nghĩa giảm tổng giờ làm.
- Nhầm writer và critics: Critics lo về staffing, còn tác giả yêu cầu đánh giá toàn diện.
- Suy luận vượt evidence: Trial thành công ở một công ty không chứng minh mọi ngành đều áp dụng được.
