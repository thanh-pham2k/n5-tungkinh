# Lesson 40 — The Ageing Population

Focus: Main Ideas · Matching Headings · Social Solutions

### Reading Passage

Adapting to an Older Society

A. In some countries, the proportion of older people is increasing as people live longer and birth rates remain relatively low. This demographic change affects the balance between age groups and raises questions about employment, healthcare, housing, and community support. However, an ageing population should not be viewed only as a financial burden.

B. Healthcare systems may face growing demand for services associated with later life. Some older adults require regular medical support, while others remain active and independent. Planning therefore needs to reflect differences within the older population rather than assuming that everyone above a particular age has identical needs.

C. Employment is another area of debate. Some employers believe that retaining experienced workers can preserve valuable knowledge and reduce skill shortages. Others worry about the costs of adapting workplaces or training employees in new technologies. Supportive policies may include flexible schedules and opportunities for continued learning, depending on individual circumstances.

D. The design of communities also matters. Accessible transport, safe pavements, and housing that accommodates changing mobility needs can help older residents remain independent. These improvements may benefit younger people and those with disabilities as well. Nevertheless, providing facilities is not enough if residents cannot afford or easily reach them.

E. An effective response to population ageing requires more than increasing healthcare budgets. Governments and communities should consider employment opportunities, accessible environments, and social participation. Older residents have different abilities and preferences, and policies are more likely to succeed when they recognise this diversity.

### Paraphrasing

- proportion of older people → share of elderly residents
- regular medical support → continuing healthcare assistance
- retaining experienced workers → keeping knowledgeable employees
- changing mobility needs → evolving requirements for movement and access
- recognise this diversity → acknowledge different abilities and preferences

### Common Mistakes

- Main Idea: Ageing population không chỉ là vấn đề healthcare costs.
- Matching Headings: Đoạn C so sánh hai quan điểm về older workers, không chỉ nói flexible schedules.
- Inference: Có facilities không đồng nghĩa người dân có đủ khả năng chi trả hoặc tiếp cận.
