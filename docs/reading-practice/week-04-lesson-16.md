# Lesson 16 — The Importance of Sleep

Focus: Identifying Details · Summary Completion · Cause and Effect

### Reading Passage

Why Sleep Is More Than Rest

A. Sleep is often treated as time taken away from work or study, especially in societies that value productivity. However, sleep is an active biological process rather than a period when the brain simply stops working. During sleep, the body carries out important functions that support learning, emotional balance, and physical recovery. The amount of sleep people need varies, but regularly ignoring tiredness can affect everyday performance.

B. One important function of sleep involves memory. During the day, people encounter large amounts of information. Sleep helps the brain organize and strengthen some of these experiences. A student who studies late into the night may gain extra revision time, but losing sleep can make it harder to remember or apply what was learned. Therefore, spending more hours studying does not automatically produce better academic results.

C. Sleep also influences emotional responses. People who have slept poorly may find ordinary difficulties more frustrating than usual. They might react impatiently during conversations or struggle to concentrate on demanding tasks. These effects can create additional problems at school or work, even when the original cause is insufficient rest rather than a lack of ability.

D. Modern habits can make regular sleep difficult. Late-night entertainment, irregular schedules, and frequent phone notifications may delay bedtime. Some people attempt to recover by sleeping much longer at weekends. Although extra rest may help them feel better, large differences between weekday and weekend schedules can make consistent sleep patterns harder to maintain.

E. Improving sleep habits does not always require complicated solutions. Keeping relatively regular sleeping hours, reducing unnecessary interruptions, and allowing enough time for rest can support better routines. The central lesson is that sleep should be considered part of effective learning and daily functioning, not simply the reward that follows completed work.

### Paraphrasing

- regularly ignoring tiredness → repeatedly failing to respond to the need for rest
- strengthen some experiences → reinforce certain memories
- harder to remember or apply → more difficult to recall or use
- ordinary difficulties more frustrating → everyday problems harder to tolerate
- relatively regular sleeping hours → fairly consistent sleep schedules

### Common Mistakes

- Nhầm nguyên nhân và kết quả: Thiếu ngủ có thể gây mất tập trung, không nhất thiết do người đó thiếu năng lực.
- Nhầm False và Not Given: Không có dữ liệu về số giờ ngủ trung bình của học sinh.
- Chọn theo từ khóa: Học nhiều giờ hơn không đồng nghĩa kết quả tốt hơn.
