# Lesson 44 — Small Businesses

Focus: Table Completion · Comparing Trends · Business Decisions

### Reading Passage

How Small Businesses Adapt to Change

A. Small businesses often operate with limited staff and financial resources. They may respond quickly to customer preferences because owners are closely involved in daily operations. However, they can also be vulnerable when costs rise or demand changes unexpectedly. Careful decisions are therefore important for maintaining stable operations.

B. A fictional neighbourhood bakery recorded its monthly sales across three channels. In 2023, in-store purchases generated $12,000, online orders generated $3,000, and local delivery partnerships generated $5,000. By 2025, in-store sales reached $13,000, online orders rose to $7,000, and delivery partnerships increased to $6,000. Total monthly sales grew from $20,000 to $26,000.

C. The bakery owner noticed that customers increasingly valued convenient ordering. However, online growth also created additional packaging and service costs. A rise in sales did not automatically produce the same increase in profit. The owner therefore reviewed costs before expanding the online operation further.

D. Customer feedback influenced another decision. Some regular visitors wanted more freshly prepared products, while online buyers requested clearer delivery times. Rather than introducing many new items, the bakery improved production schedules and provided more accurate order information. These changes aimed to address different customer needs without creating unnecessary complexity.

E. Small-business success depends on more than rapid sales growth. Owners need to compare revenue with costs, understand customer behaviour, and decide which improvements are sustainable. The bakery's experience illustrates how combining financial information with customer feedback can support better decisions.

### Paraphrasing

- limited financial resources → restricted business funding
- sales channel → method through which customers purchase
- additional service costs → extra operating expenses
- customer preferences → buyers' needs and priorities
- sustainable improvements → changes that can be maintained

### Common Mistakes

- Revenue ≠ Profit: Doanh thu tăng không chứng minh lợi nhuận tăng tương ứng.
- Nhầm tổng với chênh lệch: $26,000 là tổng 2025; $6,000 là mức tăng.
- Comparing Trends: Online tăng nhanh nhất về số tiền nhưng chưa phải kênh có doanh thu lớn nhất.
