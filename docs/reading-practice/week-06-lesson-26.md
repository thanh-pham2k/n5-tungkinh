# Lesson 26 — Working from Home

Focus: Writer's Opinion · Comparing Viewpoints · Inference

### Reading Passage

Is Working from Home Better for Everyone?

A. Working from home has become an established option in many office-based industries. Supporters argue that removing daily journeys saves time and allows employees greater control over their schedules. Some workers also find it easier to concentrate away from a busy office. However, these benefits depend on home conditions, job responsibilities, and individual preferences.

B. Managers express different opinions about remote work. Some believe that employees can be judged fairly by the quality of their results rather than the number of hours spent in an office. Others worry that informal conversations, which often help colleagues exchange ideas, become less frequent online. These managers do not necessarily oppose remote work, but they question whether digital meetings can replace every benefit of working together.

C. Employees also experience remote work differently. A worker with a quiet room and reliable internet may enjoy considerable independence. Someone sharing a small apartment with family members may face frequent interruptions. Remote arrangements can therefore create unequal experiences even when employees receive the same official flexibility.

D. Hybrid work, which combines home and office days, is sometimes presented as a compromise. It may preserve opportunities for collaboration while allowing some independent work at home. Nevertheless, hybrid schedules introduce challenges of their own. Teams must coordinate attendance, and workers may still travel to the office for meetings that could have taken place online.

E. The most convincing approach is not to treat remote work as either a universal improvement or an obvious mistake. Employers should consider the nature of tasks, the needs of employees, and how performance is assessed. Flexibility is valuable, but its success depends on thoughtful implementation rather than location alone.

### Paraphrasing

- removing daily journeys → eliminating regular commuting
- judged by the quality of results → assessed according to work outcomes
- informal conversations become less frequent → spontaneous exchanges happen less often
- unequal experiences → differences in working conditions and benefits
- thoughtful implementation → careful planning and execution

### Common Mistakes

- Nhầm quan điểm: Một số managers lo ngại collaboration không có nghĩa tác giả phản đối remote work.
- Yes/No/Not Given: Dự báo ba ngày làm từ xa không được đề cập nên là Not Given.
- Inference: Không quy kết người làm việc tại nhà kém hiệu quả là thiếu kỷ luật khi passage chỉ nêu điều kiện môi trường.
