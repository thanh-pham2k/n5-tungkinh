# Lesson 39 — Life in Big Cities

Focus: Matching Headings · Comparing Information · Inference

### Reading Passage

The Contradictions of City Life

A. Large cities attract residents with promises of employment, education, entertainment, and cultural variety. People may have access to opportunities that are less common in smaller communities. However, these advantages do not mean that every resident experiences city life in the same way.

B. One attraction is the variety of services. Large cities may offer specialised hospitals, universities, and workplaces within a relatively small area. Yet access is not distributed equally. Residents with higher incomes may live near convenient facilities, while others spend substantial time travelling to reach similar opportunities.

C. City life can also influence social relationships. Some people enjoy meeting individuals from different backgrounds and participating in cultural activities. Others feel isolated despite living among thousands of neighbours. Frequent contact with strangers does not necessarily create meaningful connections, particularly when long working hours leave little time for community activities.

D. The physical environment creates additional differences. Parks and pedestrian areas can provide places to rest and exercise, while heavy traffic and limited green space may make neighbourhoods less pleasant. Improving public areas can benefit residents, but planners must ensure that improvements are accessible rather than concentrated only in wealthy districts.

E. Comparing big-city life with life in smaller towns is therefore more complicated than counting available services. Smaller communities may offer quieter surroundings and stronger local familiarity, although specialised opportunities can be more limited. The most useful question is how well a place meets residents' needs, not whether one type of settlement is universally superior.

### Paraphrasing

- cultural variety → diverse cultural experiences
- access is not distributed equally → opportunities are unevenly available
- meaningful connections → close and valuable relationships
- less pleasant neighbourhoods → less comfortable living environments
- universally superior → better for everyone in all circumstances

### Common Mistakes

- Heading quá hẹp: Đoạn C không chỉ nói loneliness mà đối chiếu cơ hội kết nối và isolation.
- Main Idea: Nhiều dịch vụ không đồng nghĩa mọi cư dân đều tiếp cận được.
- Inference: Không khẳng định city hoặc town luôn tốt hơn cho tất cả mọi người.
