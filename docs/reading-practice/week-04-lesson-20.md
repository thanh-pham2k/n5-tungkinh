# Lesson 20 — The Effects of Fast Food

Focus: Summary Completion · Cause and Effect · Evaluating Trade-offs

### Reading Passage

Convenience at a Cost?

A. Fast food has become a familiar part of modern eating habits. Restaurants offering quickly prepared meals are common in shopping areas, transport centres, and busy neighbourhoods. Their popularity is partly explained by convenience, predictable prices, and limited waiting times. For people balancing work, study, and family responsibilities, these advantages can make fast food an attractive option.

B. However, regularly relying on fast food may make it harder to maintain a balanced diet. Some meals contain large amounts of salt, added sugar, or energy relative to their portion size. Others provide limited variety in vegetables and other ingredients. Nevertheless, nutritional quality differs between products, and eating fast food occasionally does not necessarily create the same concerns as depending on it for most meals.

C. Marketing and availability can also influence food choices. Attractive photographs, special offers, and convenient locations may encourage purchases that were not originally planned. When people are tired or short of time, they may pay more attention to immediate convenience than to the overall balance of their diet. This does not mean consumers have no choice, but their decisions occur within environments that influence behaviour.

D. Some restaurants have introduced smaller portions, clearer nutritional information, or more varied menu options. These changes may help customers make informed decisions. However, providing information does not guarantee that everyone will use it. Price, taste, habits, and time constraints can continue to influence purchasing behaviour.

E. Improving eating habits therefore requires more than simply telling people to avoid fast food. Planning convenient alternatives, understanding nutritional information, and making gradual changes may be more realistic. The broader lesson is that food decisions reflect both individual choices and the conditions in which those choices are made.

### Paraphrasing

- limited waiting times → short periods before receiving food
- regularly relying on fast food → frequently depending on quickly prepared meals
- purchases that were not originally planned → unplanned buying decisions
- make informed decisions → choose using relevant information
- conditions in which choices are made → circumstances influencing decisions

### Common Mistakes

- Nhầm nguyên nhân và kết quả: Quảng cáo và convenience có thể tác động đến quyết định mua, không có nghĩa khách hàng mất hoàn toàn quyền lựa chọn.
- Nhầm False và Not Given: Passage không có số liệu giảm năng lượng tiêu thụ sau khi thay đổi khẩu phần.
- Khái quát quá mức: Không phải mọi fast food đều có giá trị dinh dưỡng giống nhau.
