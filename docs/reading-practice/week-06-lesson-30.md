# Lesson 30 — Work-Life Balance

Focus: Writer's Opinion · Comparing Viewpoints · Argument Evaluation

### Reading Passage

Finding a Sustainable Balance

A. Work-life balance is often described as dividing time fairly between employment and personal activities. Yet equal hours do not necessarily create equal satisfaction. Some people enjoy demanding careers and willingly devote considerable time to them, while others need predictable schedules to manage family responsibilities. Balance therefore involves more than calculating the number of hours spent at work.

B. One viewpoint emphasizes individual responsibility. Supporters argue that workers can improve balance by organizing tasks, setting priorities, and learning to refuse unnecessary commitments. These strategies may help, but employees do not always control deadlines, staffing levels, or expectations from managers.

C. Another viewpoint places greater responsibility on employers. Advocates argue that realistic workloads, reasonable communication policies, and flexible arrangements can reduce unnecessary pressure. Critics respond that employers must also meet customer needs and maintain business performance. A policy that benefits one group of workers may create additional demands for colleagues providing essential coverage.

D. Technology adds complexity. Digital tools allow some employees to work at convenient times and locations, but they can also blur the boundary between employment and personal life. A worker who checks messages throughout the evening may feel unable to disconnect. The same technology can therefore support flexibility while making genuine rest more difficult.

E. A sustainable approach requires shared responsibility and attention to context. Individuals can establish useful routines, but organizations must also create conditions in which those routines are realistic. Neither personal discipline nor workplace policy alone can solve every difficulty. Effective balance depends on matching expectations with available time, responsibilities, and opportunities for recovery.

### Paraphrasing

- dividing time fairly → allocating time appropriately
- individual responsibility → personal accountability
- realistic workloads → manageable job demands
- blur the boundary → make the separation less clear
- shared responsibility → duties divided between different parties

### Common Mistakes

- Nhầm writer với critics: Critics lo về business performance; tác giả không kết luận employee well-being phải đứng sau khách hàng.
- Yes/No/Not Given: Không có quy định ngừng trả lời tin nhắn lúc 6 p.m.
- Writer's Opinion: Work-life balance không nhất thiết là chia thời gian 50/50.
