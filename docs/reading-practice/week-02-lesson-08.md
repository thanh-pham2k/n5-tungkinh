# Lesson 08 — Smartphones and Communication

Focus: Sentence Completion · Paraphrasing · Comparison

### Reading Passage

When Communication Becomes Constant

A. Smartphones have made communication faster and more accessible than ever before. People can send messages, share photographs, or make video calls from almost anywhere with a suitable connection. This convenience has changed expectations about how quickly others should respond. While immediate contact can be valuable, constant availability may also create pressure.

B. One clear benefit is the ability to maintain relationships across long distances. Family members living in different countries can share ordinary moments through video conversations. Friends can organize activities without arranging meetings in advance. However, digital contact does not always provide the same experience as spending time together physically. Facial expressions, pauses, and other social signals may be harder to interpret through short written messages.

C. Smartphones have also influenced workplace communication. Employees can respond to urgent questions while travelling or working remotely. This flexibility may improve coordination, particularly when colleagues are in different locations. Yet messages arriving outside working hours can blur the boundary between professional and personal life. Some workers feel obliged to reply immediately even when no urgent response is required.

D. Another concern involves divided attention. During a face-to-face conversation, checking a phone repeatedly may signal that the other person is not receiving full attention. The interruption can weaken the quality of the interaction, even if the phone user intends no disrespect. Some people therefore put their devices away during meals or important discussions.

E. Smartphones are neither automatically helpful nor harmful to communication. Their effects depend on social expectations and individual habits. Setting reasonable boundaries, choosing suitable communication methods, and giving others undivided attention can help people enjoy the benefits of connectivity without allowing it to control every interaction.

### Paraphrasing

- maintain relationships across long distances → stay connected despite geographical separation
- blur the boundary → make the distinction less clear
- feel obliged to reply → feel responsible for responding
- receiving full attention → being listened to without distraction
- undivided attention → complete concentration on one interaction

### Common Mistakes

- Nhầm nguyên nhân và kết quả: Thông báo ngoài giờ có thể tạo áp lực trả lời, không phải vì mọi thông báo đều khẩn cấp.
- Nhầm phương tiện và chất lượng: Liên lạc thường xuyên không bảo đảm giao tiếp hiệu quả.
- Suy luận quá mức: Bài không nói video calls phổ biến hơn tin nhắn.
