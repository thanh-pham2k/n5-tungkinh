# Lesson 02 — The Benefits of Reading

Focus: Supporting Details · Matching Headings · Inference

### Reading Passage

Why Reading Still Matters

A. Reading is often associated with school examinations, but its value extends beyond academic achievement. People read to gain information, explore unfamiliar experiences, and enjoy stories. Although digital media now competes for attention, reading remains an important activity because it encourages sustained engagement with ideas. The benefits, however, depend partly on what people read and how they approach the activity.

B. One advantage of regular reading is vocabulary development. Readers encounter words in meaningful situations rather than isolated lists. For example, a novel may introduce an unfamiliar expression through a character's conversation, allowing readers to guess its meaning from context. Repeated exposure can strengthen understanding. Nevertheless, simply seeing a word once does not guarantee that someone will remember or use it correctly.

C. Reading can also support critical thinking. When readers examine an argument, they may consider whether the evidence is convincing or whether another explanation is possible. This process is especially useful when reading news articles and opinion pieces. However, readers do not automatically become critical thinkers. They need to question assumptions and compare information rather than accept every statement as fact.

D. Fiction offers a different kind of experience. Stories introduce characters whose backgrounds, decisions, and emotions may differ from those of readers. Following these characters can encourage people to consider unfamiliar perspectives. Yet fiction does not necessarily produce the same response in everyone. Personal experiences and interpretations influence how readers understand a story.

E. Developing a reading habit can be difficult when daily schedules are crowded. Some people find it easier to read for fifteen minutes each evening than to set ambitious monthly targets. Choosing interesting material may also encourage consistency. Ultimately, reading is most valuable when it becomes an active and sustainable practice rather than an occasional task completed only for assessment.

### Paraphrasing

- beyond academic achievement → not limited to success at school
- meaningful situations → contexts that provide useful clues
- question assumptions → examine beliefs that may not be justified
- unfamiliar perspectives → viewpoints different from one's own
- encourage consistency → help maintain a regular routine

### Common Mistakes

- Đánh đồng lợi ích với kết quả chắc chắn: Reading có thể hỗ trợ critical thinking, không tự động tạo ra kỹ năng này.
- Nhầm supporting detail với main idea: Ví dụ đọc 15 phút chỉ minh họa cách xây dựng thói quen.
- Suy luận quá mức: Không có bằng chứng rằng fiction có tác động giống nhau với tất cả độc giả.
