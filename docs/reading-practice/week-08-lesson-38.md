# Lesson 38 — Population Growth

Focus: Main Ideas · Comparing Information · Problem and Solution

### Reading Passage

When Populations Change

A. Population growth occurs when the number of people in an area increases over time. Changes can result from births, deaths, and migration. However, population patterns differ widely between regions. Some cities grow rapidly as people arrive for work, while other communities experience slow growth or declining numbers.

B. Growing populations can support economic activity by increasing the number of workers and consumers. Businesses may find new customers, and governments may collect more revenue to fund services. Yet these opportunities depend on whether employment and infrastructure expand sufficiently. When growth is faster than investment, schools, hospitals, and housing can become overcrowded.

C. The age structure of a population is as important as its total size. A community with many children may need additional schools and childcare services. A region with a rising proportion of older residents may face greater demand for accessible housing and support services. Two places with equal populations can therefore have very different planning priorities.

D. Migration can help address labour shortages in some areas, but it may also increase short-term demand for accommodation and transport. Policies that focus only on population totals may overlook the skills, needs, and circumstances of new residents. Effective planning requires understanding how population change affects particular communities.

E. Population growth should not be judged as entirely positive or negative. Its consequences depend on the speed of change, the characteristics of residents, and the capacity of public systems. Governments can respond more effectively by using demographic information to anticipate needs rather than waiting until shortages become severe.

### Paraphrasing

- declining numbers → a shrinking population
- age structure → distribution of residents across age groups
- overcrowded services → public facilities facing excessive demand
- labour shortages → insufficient workers
- anticipate needs → prepare for expected requirements

### Common Mistakes

- Population size vs Age structure: Hai nơi cùng dân số vẫn có nhu cầu dịch vụ khác nhau.
- Main Idea: Population growth không được mô tả là hoàn toàn tốt hoặc xấu.
- Problem and Solution: Tăng lao động cần đi cùng quy hoạch nhà ở và giao thông.
