# Lesson 13 — Renewable Energy

Focus: Matching Information · Problem and Solution · Comparison

### Reading Passage

Building a Cleaner Energy System

A. Renewable energy comes from sources that are naturally replenished, such as sunlight, wind, and moving water. These technologies can reduce dependence on fossil fuels and lower emissions from electricity generation. However, replacing traditional energy systems involves technical and economic challenges. Producing clean electricity is only one part of ensuring that power remains available when people need it.

B. Solar panels generate electricity when sunlight reaches their surfaces, while wind turbines depend on suitable wind conditions. Their output can therefore vary throughout the day or across seasons. This creates difficulties when electricity demand does not match production. A city may require considerable power in the evening, for instance, when solar generation has already declined.

C. Energy storage can help address this mismatch. Batteries can store electricity produced during periods of high generation and release it later. Another approach is to connect different regions through stronger electricity networks, allowing areas with surplus power to support places experiencing shortages. Nevertheless, storage equipment and network improvements require investment and careful planning.

D. Renewable projects may also create local environmental concerns. Large solar installations require land, and poorly located wind farms can affect birds or nearby communities. These impacts do not necessarily outweigh the benefits of cleaner energy, but they demonstrate the importance of choosing suitable locations and consulting affected residents.

E. Governments and energy companies are exploring combinations of renewable sources, storage, and more efficient electricity use. Rather than relying on one technology, a diversified system can respond to changing conditions. The transition will depend not only on installing more equipment but also on developing networks, policies, and practices that support reliable electricity supplies.

### Paraphrasing

- naturally replenished → restored through natural processes
- output can vary → production may fluctuate
- periods of high generation → times when substantial electricity is produced
- surplus power → electricity exceeding local needs
- a diversified system → a system combining different energy sources

- Matching Information: Đoạn C nói cả batteries và kết nối vùng; cần nhận diện ý chuyển điện giữa thời gian và địa điểm.
