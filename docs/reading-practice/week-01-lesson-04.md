# Lesson 04 — Learning a Second Language

Focus: Scanning · Matching Information · True/False/Not Given

### Reading Passage

More Than Memorising Words

A. Learning another language is often described as a way to communicate with people from different countries. While communication is an important goal, language study can also introduce learners to unfamiliar cultural practices and ways of expressing ideas. Yet progress rarely follows a straight line. Learners may improve quickly in one skill while continuing to struggle with another.

B. Vocabulary and grammar provide a foundation, but knowing rules does not always lead to fluent conversation. A learner might understand how to form a question yet hesitate when speaking to a stranger. Real communication requires quick decisions, listening carefully, and responding to unexpected information. Regular opportunities to use the language can help learners connect classroom knowledge with practical situations.

C. Mistakes play an important role in this process. Some students avoid speaking because they fear embarrassment. However, a conversation containing errors may still provide useful feedback. When teachers respond constructively, learners can recognize problems without losing confidence. This does not mean that accuracy is unimportant; rather, improvement requires both practice and correction.

D. Motivation also influences progress. Students who connect language learning to personal interests, such as music, travel, or friendships, may find it easier to maintain regular practice. Clear short-term goals can make a demanding task feel manageable. For instance, learning to order a meal confidently may provide a more immediate sense of achievement than aiming vaguely to become fluent.

E. Digital tools offer additional opportunities through videos, vocabulary applications, and online conversations. Nevertheless, their effectiveness depends on how learners use them. Repeating isolated words on an application may improve recognition without developing conversational ability. A balanced approach combines deliberate study with meaningful communication. Ultimately, learning a language is a gradual process requiring patience, useful feedback, and consistent engagement.

### Common Mistakes

- Nhầm False và Not Given: Tác giả nói accuracy quan trọng, nhưng không có số liệu so sánh kết quả thi giữa các phương pháp.
- Hiểu sai main idea: Bài không chỉ bàn về ứng dụng học ngôn ngữ.
- Suy luận quá mức: Mục tiêu nhỏ giúp duy trì động lực, không bảo đảm người học thành thạo mọi tình huống.
