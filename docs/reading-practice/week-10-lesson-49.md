# Lesson 49 — Tourism and Local Culture

Focus: Comparing Information · True/False/Not Given · Problem and Solution

### Reading Passage

When Visitors Change a Place

A. Tourism can create opportunities for communities with distinctive cultural traditions. Visitors may purchase local products, attend festivals, and support businesses that provide accommodation or food. Income from tourism can help maintain cultural activities. However, growing visitor numbers may also change how traditions are performed and experienced.

B. In a fictional coastal town, attendance at a cultural festival increased from 8,000 visitors in 2021 to 14,000 in 2025. During the same period, the number of stalls selling locally made crafts rose from 25 to 40. Organisers welcomed the additional income, but some residents complained that performances had become shorter to accommodate more tourist groups.

C. Businesses and residents sometimes evaluate tourism differently. Shop owners may welcome longer opening hours because they create additional sales. Families living near busy attractions may prefer quieter evenings. These preferences are not necessarily incompatible, but they require planning rather than assuming that economic growth benefits everyone equally.

D. Some communities have introduced visitor limits at sensitive locations or organised smaller cultural events throughout the year. These measures can reduce pressure during peak periods, although they may also require businesses to adjust their operations. Involving residents in decisions can help identify which cultural activities should remain primarily community-focused.

E. The author argues that tourism should support local culture rather than determine its entire direction. Visitor spending can be valuable, but cultural traditions also have purposes unrelated to commercial demand. Sustainable tourism depends on balancing economic opportunities with residents' quality of life and cultural control.

### Paraphrasing

- distinctive cultural traditions → unique local customs
- additional income → increased earnings
- accommodate more tourist groups → serve a greater number of visitor groups
- residents' quality of life → everyday well-being of local people
- cultural control → influence over cultural decisions

### Common Mistakes

- Nhầm đơn vị: 14,000 là visitors; 40 là craft stalls.
- False vs Not Given: Performance ngắn hơn là thông tin có thể kiểm chứng; visitor tax không được nhắc đến.
- Inference: Doanh thu tăng không đồng nghĩa mọi cư dân đều hưởng lợi.
