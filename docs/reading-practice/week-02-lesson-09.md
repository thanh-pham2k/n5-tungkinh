# Lesson 09 — Robots in the Workplace

Focus: Cause and Effect · Paraphrasing · Matching Information

### Reading Passage

Working Alongside Machines

A. Robots are increasingly used in workplaces that require repetitive or physically demanding tasks. In factories, automated machines can assemble products, move materials, and inspect certain components. Similar technologies are appearing in warehouses and other industries. Their development has created both optimism about productivity and concern about the future of employment.

B. One major advantage is consistency. Robots can repeat programmed movements without becoming physically tired in the same way humans do. In environments involving dangerous temperatures or heavy equipment, machines may also reduce workers' exposure to hazards. However, robots still require maintenance and supervision. A mechanical failure can interrupt production, particularly when several processes depend on the same machine.

C. Automation can change the types of jobs available. Some routine positions may disappear or require fewer employees. At the same time, businesses may need technicians who can repair machines, monitor systems, and solve unexpected problems. These new roles often demand different skills, meaning workers may require additional training rather than simply moving directly into another position.

D. Introducing robots can also involve significant costs. Businesses must purchase equipment, adapt working spaces, and train employees. Large companies may find these investments easier to manage than smaller firms. Consequently, the benefits of automation may not be distributed equally across industries or workplaces.

E. The future of work is therefore unlikely to involve a simple choice between humans and robots. Machines may handle predictable physical tasks while people focus on judgment, communication, and complex decisions. Successful adoption depends on careful planning, worker preparation, and realistic expectations about what technology can achieve.

### Paraphrasing

- physically demanding tasks → work requiring considerable physical effort
- reduce workers' exposure to hazards → limit contact with workplace dangers
- require additional training → need to develop new abilities
- significant costs → substantial financial investment
- distributed equally → shared to a similar extent

### Common Mistakes

- Nhầm nguyên nhân và kết quả: Công việc mới đòi kỹ năng khác nên người lao động cần đào tạo lại.
- Nhầm khả năng với sự chắc chắn: May disappear không đồng nghĩa tất cả công việc sẽ biến mất.
- Nhầm False và Not Given: Bài nói doanh nghiệp nhỏ gặp khó về chi phí, không đề cập trợ cấp chính phủ.
