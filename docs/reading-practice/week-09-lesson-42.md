# Lesson 42 — Advertising and Consumers

Focus: Comparing Trends · Table Completion · Consumer Behaviour

### Reading Passage

Why Some Advertisements Influence Us

A. Advertising introduces products and encourages consumers to consider purchasing them. Businesses use different approaches, including emotional messages, product demonstrations, and recommendations from familiar personalities. However, attracting attention is not the same as convincing someone to buy. A memorable advertisement may entertain viewers without changing their behaviour.

B. A fictional study asked 400 adults which advertising format they found most useful when evaluating an unfamiliar product. In 2023, 35% selected detailed product demonstrations, 30% selected customer reviews, 20% selected influencer recommendations, and 15% selected short entertaining advertisements. In 2025, demonstrations remained first at 38%, while customer reviews rose to 34%. Influencer recommendations declined to 16%, and entertaining advertisements fell to 12%.

C. Researchers also asked respondents what made them distrust an advertisement. In 2025, 55% mentioned exaggerated claims, compared with 40% in 2023. Participants explained that advertisements promising unrealistic results could create doubt even when the product itself was useful. Clear explanations and realistic examples were often considered more credible.

D. Advertising effects can differ between audiences. Someone unfamiliar with a product may value a demonstration that explains its functions. A returning customer may pay greater attention to reviews about reliability. These differences suggest that an advertisement's usefulness depends partly on what consumers already know and what information they need.

E. Businesses should therefore distinguish attention from trust. Entertaining content can introduce a brand, but evidence and accurate claims may be more important when consumers evaluate a purchase. The fictional study does not prove that every consumer behaves alike; it illustrates how preferences and concerns may change over time.

### Paraphrasing

- attract attention → capture viewers' interest
- convincing someone to buy → persuading a customer to purchase
- exaggerated claims → overstated promises
- unfamiliar product → item the consumer does not know well
- advertising preferences → preferred promotional formats

### Common Mistakes

- Attention ≠ Purchase: Quảng cáo đáng nhớ không nhất thiết tạo ra đơn hàng.
- Nhầm mức giảm: Influencer giảm 4 điểm, entertaining ads giảm 3 điểm.
- Inference: Kết quả khảo sát không đại diện chắc chắn cho mọi người tiêu dùng.
