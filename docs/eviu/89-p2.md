## Bài tập sách — Đáp án

### 89.1 — Phân loại tính từ

| Gradable adjectives | Extreme adjectives |
| --- | --- |
| bad | dreadful |
| important | essential |
| small | tiny |
| tired | exhausted |
| frightened | terrified |

### 89.2 — Viết lại email với tính từ mạnh hơn

Đáp án mẫu:

> Dear Sandy,
>
> Arrived on Sunday evening after an **absolutely exhausting** journey. We're **delighted** with the hotel: our room is **huge**, and the food is **delicious**. We've been lucky with the weather as well. The first day was wet but the last three days have been **marvellous**.
>
> Tomorrow we're going to walk the coastal path to Dartmouth Castle. It's quite a difficult route and people tell us it's **absolutely essential** to take a map, but it sounds **really fascinating**, so I'm looking forward to it.
>
> I'll write again in a couple of days and tell you all about it.
>
> Love,
> Benita

## TL;DR — Ghi nhớ

- **Gradable adjectives:** dùng very hoặc really → very tired, really good.
- **Extreme adjectives:** dùng absolutely hoặc really → absolutely exhausted, really fascinating.
- **-ing:** mô tả thứ gây cảm xúc → boring, confusing, terrifying.
- **-ed:** mô tả người chịu tác động → bored, confused, terrified.
- Các từ quan trọng: **marvellous, awful, tiny, huge, fascinating, exhausted, essential, delicious, delighted**.

**Quan trọng nhất:** -ing thường chỉ **nguyên nhân gây cảm xúc**; -ed chỉ **người cảm nhận cảm xúc đó**.
