### 84.2 — Thay thế bằng từ hoặc cụm tương đương

1. He **got very angry** when he saw me.
2. Hi Sue. **How are things?**
3. Could you **fetch** my handbag from the other room?
4. Do you want to **continue** and do the next exercise?
5. **What's happening** in the next classroom?
6. When I got back, the others **had left**.
7. I can't stay for the weekend; I have to **return** on Friday.
8. Excuse me. Does this road **lead to** the bus station?
9. My girlfriend had fish but I **chose** the chicken dish.

### 84.4 — Over to You

Câu trả lời mẫu để luyện giao tiếp:

- **Do you often go swimming in the summer?** — Yes, I sometimes go swimming with my friends.
- **Do you go clubbing most weekends?** — No, I prefer staying at home.
- **Do you often go shopping with a friend?** — Yes, we sometimes go shopping together.
- **Did you often go for picnics as a child?** — Yes, my family often went for picnics.
- **Do you often go for a walk on your own?** — Yes, I go for a walk every evening.
- **Do you like going out for a meal?** — Yes, especially at weekends.
- **Do you often go sightseeing on holiday?** — Yes, I enjoy visiting new places.
- **Do you enjoy going out for a drive in the country?** — Yes, I find it relaxing.

## TL;DR — Ghi nhớ

- **Go + V-ing:** hoạt động giải trí → go shopping, swimming, sightseeing, clubbing.
- **Go for a + noun:** đi làm hoạt động → go for a walk, drive, drink, meal, picnic.
- **Go + adjective:** thay đổi trạng thái → go grey, bald, deaf, blind, mad.
- **Go on:** tiếp tục hoặc xảy ra; **go out:** đèn tắt; **go wrong:** xảy ra sự cố.
- **Go well / badly:** diễn ra tốt / không tốt.
- **Go away:** đi nghỉ; **go for:** lựa chọn; **go and get:** đi lấy mang về.

**Quan trọng nhất:** Học **go** theo cấu trúc và ngữ cảnh để phân biệt nghĩa chính xác.
